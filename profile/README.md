# Saiko

A harm reduction journal for iPhone, built to be calm, honest and private.

- **Log quickly:** exact, estimated or unknown amounts, with mixes, notes and check-ins during a session.
- **Know where you are:** a timeline showing onset, peak and comedown for what you logged.
- **Check combinations:** interactions between substances and your regular medicines, with clear wording when something isn't checked.
- **See the sources:** dose and duration info credits where it comes from (PsychonautWiki, TripSit and others). When data is missing, Saiko says so instead of guessing.
- **Private by design:** no account and no analytics, with optional app lock and encrypted backups. Your journal never leaves your phone unless you export it.

Saiko is in development. The open reference catalogue is at saiko-app/catalogue.
